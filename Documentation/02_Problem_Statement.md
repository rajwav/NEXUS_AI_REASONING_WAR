# 02. PROBLEM STATEMENT

## 1. Context and Problem Formulation
In modern computer science and artificial intelligence education, a significant pedagogical gap exists between mathematical abstraction and embodied system engineering. Students and researchers frequently encounter AI algorithms (such as graph pathfinders, CSP constraint solvers, production rule engines, and minimax game trees) as isolated assignments evaluated solely on toy benchmark metrics. Consequently, the following fundamental problems arise:

1. **Lack of Embodied Context**: Heuristics such as Manhattan vs Euclidean distance are rarely experienced in dynamic spatial navigation under hazard constraints.
2. **Abstract Failure Modes**: Constraint backtracking is typically taught via abstract N-Queens matrices rather than physical multi-frequency laser alignment or lock combinations where intermediate state violations and rollbacks have tangible system consequences.
3. **Passive Pedagogical Interfaces**: Existing educational software often resembles static dashboards or text-heavy choice menus ("Option 1 vs Option 2"), which fail to engage modern learners who benefit from spatial exploration, cause-and-effect agency, and real-time visual thought traces.
4. **Disconnection Between Classical AI and Modern Machine Learning**: Classical symbolic reasoning and statistical machine learning are frequently taught as disjoint disciplines, leaving students without an intuitive grasp of how ML telemetry classifiers can adapt symbolic puzzle complexity in real time.

---

## 2. Research and Engineering Objectives
To resolve these challenges, this project formulates the design and construction of **NEXUS AI REASONING WAR**, addressing the following engineering requirements:

- **Environmental Spatial Simulation**: Construct a 2D tile-based research campus with 6 distinct interconnected sectors, dynamic portals, security keycards, physical hazards, and interactive machinery.
- **Deep 4-Layer Puzzle Architecture**: Ensure every mission incorporates (1) Observation/Clue Gathering, (2) Logical Reasoning, (3) Formal AI Algorithm Execution, and (4) Tangible Environmental Consequences.
- **Dual Solving Paradigm**: Provide both Human Play Mode and Autonomous AI Demonstration Mode (via companion NOVA) with real-time visual thought tracing.
- **Multi-Algorithm Spectrum**: Implement verified standard-library engines for $A^*$, BFS, CSP Backtracking (with MRV, Degree heuristic, Forward Checking), Forward Chaining Inference, Minimax with $\alpha$-$\beta$ Pruning, Maximum Expected Utility (MEU), STRIPS Planning, and Edmonds-Karp Minimum Cut.
- **Online Machine Learning Pipeline**: Integrate real-time K-Means archetype clustering, Decision Tree difficulty scaling, and ANN win probability prediction directly influencing live gameplay parameters.
